# Route Proposal: Gefitinib Route A

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Invented for a demonstration. Not real laboratory results.

## Summary

Route A makes gefitinib in three steps from purchasable materials. It is the route recommended for scale-up.

## Step 2: coupling

Step 2 couples 3-chloro-4-fluoroaniline directly onto the quinazolin-4(3H)-one core.

The coupling gives 68% isolated yield at 5 g scale. No chromatography is needed. The step is expected to scale without change.

## Alternative considered

Route B reaches the same bond in two steps by displacing a 4-chloroquinazoline. It was not preferred because it needs phosphorus oxychloride, which is harder to handle.

## Open points

No batch above 5 g has been run. Safety assessment of Step 2 is still to be done.
