# Batch Record GEF-0142: Gefitinib Step 2 at 120 g

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Invented for a demonstration. Not real laboratory results.

## Batch details

Route A, Step 2 (coupling). Scale: 120 g of quinazolinone core. Run on 2026-03-03 in the kilo lab.

## Result

Isolated yield fell to 31%, against 68% at 5 g scale. A bis-arylated impurity was the main by-product, at 22 area percent by HPLC.

## Observations

Heat-up took 95 minutes, compared with 10 minutes at 5 g. All of the aniline was charged at the start, as in the 5 g procedure.

## Follow-up

Deviation DEV-2026-031 was opened. The batch failed its purity specification and was not carried forward.
