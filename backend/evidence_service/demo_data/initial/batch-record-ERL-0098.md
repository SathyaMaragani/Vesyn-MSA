# Batch Record ERL-0098: Erlotinib coupling at 80 g

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Invented for a demonstration. Not real laboratory results.

## Batch details

Erlotinib, coupling of 3-ethynylaniline onto the quinazolinone core. Scale: 80 g. Run on 2025-11-20.

## Result

Isolated yield was 35%, against 64% at laboratory scale. A bis-arylated impurity was the main by-product.

## Root cause

The root cause was recorded as known issue KI-07: over-arylation of the unprotected quinazolinone during a slow heat-up with excess aniline.

## Follow-up

No process change was made. The erlotinib campaign was paused for other reasons, and KI-07 was left open.
